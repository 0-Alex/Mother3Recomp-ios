MOTHER 3 Recomp

Run Mother3Recomp.exe to open the launcher.
Provide your own ROM and optional BIOS.
F10 toggles the bezel; F11 opens the runtime menu; Escape exits.

README.md lists current public downloads and build instructions.
Candidate 1.0.6 source changes are documented in docs/FIXES-1.0.6.md.
